This wiki contains detailed information about pets, fruits, rarities, and game mechanics in BTF 2.

Game Mechanics

BTF 2 is an RNG-based game where players roll for pets and fruits. Each item has a rarity that affects its spawn rate and value. Higher rarities are rarer and more powerful.

Rarities include: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary.

Pets

Pets are companions that provide various buffs and abilities. They come in different rarities.

Rarity Amount Description Example Pets
Common 10 Basic companions with minor buffs. Cat, Dog, Bird
Uncommon 8 Slightly better stats and effects. Wolf, Fox, Rabbit
Rare 6 Mid-tier pets with unique abilities. Lion, Tiger, Eagle
Epic 4 Powerful pets obtained through high-tier rolls. Dragon, Phoenix, Unicorn
Legendary 2 The rarest and most powerful creatures. God Beast, Celestial

Fruits

Fruits grant special powers and abilities when consumed. They also have rarities affecting their availability.

Rarity Amount Description Example Fruits
Common 12 Basic fruits with simple effects. Apple, Banana, Orange
Uncommon 10 Moderate bonuses and abilities. Grape, Pineapple, Mango
Rare 8 Stronger effects and special moves. Dragon Fruit, Star Fruit, Kiwi
Epic 5 High-value items used in crafting. Golden Apple, Mystic Berry, Shadow Fruit
Legendary 3 Extremely rare with game-changing powers. Divine Fruit, Eternal Fruit, Chaos Fruit
